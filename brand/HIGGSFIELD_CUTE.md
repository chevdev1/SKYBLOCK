# Higgsfield: cute character series (Rina, Ryzhik, Kumo)

Refs (transparent, rendered from our own models): `brand/refs-game/17-cute-trio-transparent.png`, `17-cute-duo-transparent.png`.

## Design analysis (what must stay)
- **Voxel, chunky cubes.** Heads are big cubes (about 45% of height), bodies and limbs are boxes. No smoothing, no round shapes except Kumo's eyes' highlights.
- **Face:** tall dark-navy rectangular eyes with a white square highlight (top right), small pink rectangular blush, tiny brown mouth, thin eyebrows. Always simple and frontal.
- **Rina:** red hair with two side pigtails (white hair tie on one), pink bow on top, purple shirt, yellow belt, lilac skirt block, white socks, maroon boots, cyan cape.
- **Ryzhik:** orange hair with a small tuft, blue shirt, brown belt with gold buckle, white trousers with gold trim, brown boots, pink cape.
- **Kumo:** white cube cloud body with two white arm blocks, pink-lined long bunny ears, big dark-navy eyes with two highlights, pink cheeks, tiny smile; soft lilac shading.
- **Palette:** pastel pink (#ffd3e3 / #ff4f94), sky blue, lilac, gold accents (#ffc23c). Soft, even lighting, light rim light.
